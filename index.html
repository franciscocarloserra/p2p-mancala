Mancala (Kalah)
Tap one of your pits: its stones are sown one by one toward your store, skipping the opponent's store.
If the last one lands in your store, you play again.
If it lands in an empty pit of yours with stones opposite, you capture both.
When one side is empty, each player keeps what's left on their side. Biggest store wins.
2 to 10 players: whoever opens the room first picks how many; the game starts by itself when that many have the link open. Later arrivals watch. With 3+, each row is a player, stones flow row to row, and captures take from the player halfway around the table.
